# Research-Paper Copyediting Checks

Safe mechanical fixes organized by category. Adapted from paper-revision-editor (ipeirotis).

## Safe Fixes (apply automatically)
1. Grammar & agreement — subject-verb, pronoun agreement, dangling modifiers
2. Punctuation for parsing — commas, colons, semicolons (no em-dashes)
3. Parallelism — coordinate items in lists, method steps, hypotheses
4. Abbreviations — define at first use, use consistently
5. Capitalization & hyphenation — normalize within scope
6. Units & symbols — preserve numerical values, normalize spacing
7. Tense — past for specific studies, present for established results
8. Table/figure callouts — keep logical order, do not renumber

## Consistency Risks (flag for author)
- Competing technical terms that may mark different constructs
- Field-standard capitalization (named datasets, model families)
- Spelling variants (American vs British, unknown venue)
- Statistical language changes — "significant", p-values, effect sizes

## Evidence Risks (do not edit, ask author)
- Adding facts, citations, results, limitations, or interpretations
- Changing numerical claims, statistics, p-values, sample sizes
- Altering citation keys or citation style beyond punctuation
