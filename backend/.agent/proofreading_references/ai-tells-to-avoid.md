# AI Tells and Academic House Style

Patterns to detect during Phase 1 audit and fix during Phase 2. Adapted from paper-revision-editor (ipeirotis).

## Banned Transition Words
These mark AI-generated or lazy academic filler:
- Furthermore, Moreover, Crucially, Importantly, Notably, Ultimately
- Delving (and "delve into")
- "It is important to note that", "It is worth noting that", "That said"
- "It should be mentioned that"

## Banned Promotional Adjectives
Perform certainty rather than earning it:
- important (as adjective), novel, interesting
- significant (except statistical sense), crucial, key, vital, pivotal
- comprehensive (only if literally exhaustive), holistic, multifaceted
- nuanced, rich, deep, powerful, vast (as generic intensifiers)

## Importance-Signaling Verbs
- underscores, highlights, emphasizes (when object is "importance of")
- showcases, "plays a key/central/crucial role in"

## Inflated Noun Phrases & Dead Metaphors
- "the landscape of", "the realm of", "the world of"
- "a myriad of", "a plethora of", "a wide array of"
- "rich tapestry", "mosaic of", "paradigm shift", "game-changer"
- "in today's fast-paced/data-driven/increasingly digital world"

## LLM Template Shapes
- "It is not just about X, it is about Y"
- "From X to Y, [sweeping claim]"
- "Not only ... but also ..." (more than once per section)
- "We show that..." — replace with the claim itself
- "It is well known that..." — cite or cut

## Em-dashes
Do not use em-dashes. Replace with comma, colon, parentheses, or two sentences.

## Filler Hedging
- "may potentially suggest that" — pick a confidence level
- "This paragraph discusses..." — just discuss
- "Navigate" as metaphor, "leverage" when "use" works
- "Robust" as generic positive — robust to what?
