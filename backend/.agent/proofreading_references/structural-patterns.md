# Section-Specific Structural Patterns

Diagnostic lenses for each section of an academic paper. Adapted from paper-revision-editor (ipeirotis).

## Introduction
1. Puzzle-first opening — poses a question the reader did not know to ask
2. Specific gap identified — what is missing in current knowledge
3. Contribution paragraph — what the paper does, no later than page 2
4. No textbook opening ("X is a fundamental problem")
5. No literature dump — related work belongs in its own section

## Abstract
1. Context (1-2 sentences) — field and broad question
2. Gap (1-2 sentences) — what is missing
3. Contribution (1 sentence) — "Here we...", active verb
4. Evidence (2-4 sentences) — strongest support, with numbers
5. Implications (1-2 sentences) — why this matters

Check: can a reader who reads only the abstract correctly describe the contribution?

## Methodology
1. Reproducibility first — specify data, preprocessing, model, hyperparameters, evaluation
2. Justify choices that matter — unusual choices need rationale
3. Organized for reader understanding, not project chronology
4. Avoid procedural recitation — provide structural signals

## Results
1. Claim → Evidence → Claim pattern per paragraph
2. Lead with the claim, reference figure as evidence (not figure-driven)
3. Separate result from interpretation (except in venues that blur)

## Discussion
1. Restate contribution (1 paragraph)
2. Interpret results — what do they mean?
3. Honest limitations (1-2 paragraphs)
4. Future work — specific, not generic
5. Avoid summary-only discussions or overclaiming

## Conclusion
1. Single most important take-away sentence
2. Implications for the community
3. Future directions (optional, specific)
4. NOT a summary of the abstract
5. No new claims not supported by body
