# General Humanizer Skill (29-Pattern Taxonomy)

This skill removes signs of AI-generated text to make writing sound organic, varied, and natural.

## Core Process & Self-Audit Loop

1. **Scan & Deconstruct**: Identify AI patterns from the 29-pattern taxonomy below.
2. **Rebuild Mechanism**: Do not merely swap synonyms. Rebuild sentences focusing on concrete technical/logical mechanisms.
3. **Burstiness**: Follow long analytical sentences with short, punchy statements.
4. **Self-Audit**:
   - Ask: *"What makes this draft obviously AI-generated?"*
   - List remaining tells.
   - Re-draft to remove those tells before producing final output.

## Key AI Patterns to Eliminate (29 Taxonomy Highlights)

1. **Undue Emphasis on Significance**: Remove statements like *stands as a testament*, *marks a pivotal moment*, *evolving landscape*.
2. **Superficial -ing Endings**: Delete tacked-on participle phrases like *"...reflecting deep connection, showcasing technical skill."*
3. **Copula Avoidance**: Replace *serves as / stands as / represents* with simple *is / are*.
4. **Negative Parallelisms & Tailing Negations**: Avoid *Not only X, but also Y* and tailing fragments like *", no guessing"*.
5. **Rule of Three**: Avoid forced triplets (*innovation, inspiration, and insights*).
6. **Synonym Cycling**: Avoid elegant variation where 4 different words refer to the same object.
7. **Signposting**: Remove *Let's dive in*, *Here's what you need to know*.
8. **Persuasive Authority Tropes**: Remove *The real question is*, *At its core*.
9. **Em Dash & Formatting Overuse**: Replace mechanical em-dashes (`—`) with commas or periods. Use straight quotes (`"..."`).

## Output Format
Output ONLY the clean, humanized text directly without conversational intro/outro commentary.
