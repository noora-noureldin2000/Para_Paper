# General Humanizer Skill

This skill removes signs of AI-generated text to make writing sound more natural, human-written, and varied.

## AI Writing Patterns to Eliminate

### 1. AI Vocabulary & Clichés
Remove or replace overused words:
* *actually, additionally, align with, crucial, delve, emphasizing, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, highlight, interplay, intricate, key (as adjective), landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore, valuable, vibrant*.
* Avoid *stands as/serves as/marks/represents a* in place of simple verbs like *is* or *are*.

### 2. Punctuation & Formatting Overuse
* **Em Dash Overuse**: Replace mechanical em dashes (`—`) with commas, periods, or parentheses where appropriate.
* **Boldface/Emojis**: Avoid excessive bolding or emoji decorations in bullet points or headings.
* **Curly Quotes**: Use straight quotes (`"..."`) instead of curly quotes (`“...”`).

### 3. Syntax & Style Patterns
* **Superficial -ing Endings**: Avoid tacked-on participle phrases like *"...reflecting the community's deep connection, showcasing their skill."*
* **Rule of Three**: Avoid forcing items or ideas into arbitrary triplets (*"innovation, inspiration, and insights"*).
* **Negative Parallelisms**: Avoid structures like *"Not only X, but also Y"* or *"It's not just about X, it's about Y"*.
* **Filler & Hedging**: Convert *"in order to"* to *"to"*, *"due to the fact that"* to *"because"*, and remove excess hedging like *"could potentially possibly be"*.

### 4. Soul and Rhythm
* **Varied Sentence Length**: Combine short and punchy sentences with longer, flowing ones. Avoid uniform sentence structures.
* **Natural Structure**: Avoid rigid outline structures and generic positive conclusions (e.g. *"the future looks bright..."*).

---

## Output Format
Rewrite the draft directly without conversational intro/outro phrases (no "Here is the rewritten text" or "I hope this helps"). Output ONLY the humanized text.
